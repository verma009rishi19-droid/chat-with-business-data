# Monsoon Operational Advisory: Delivery Delays & Service Disruption (July - August 2025)

**Document ID:** KB-OPS-2025-112  
**Effective Dates:** July 12, 2025 – August 25, 2025  
**Regions Affected:** Western Region (Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai) and Northern Region (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida)  
**Author:** VP of Logistics & Fleet Dispatch

## Environmental Context
Severe rainfall events, localized waterlogging, and arterial road closures triggered municipal red alerts across Mumbai and the National Capital Region (NCR) throughout mid-July and early August 2025.

## Operational Adjustments & Protocols
1. **Rider Speed Caps:** Automated geo-fencing lowered maximum permitted two-wheeler velocity from 35 km/h to 22 km/h for rider safety.
2. **Dynamic Delivery Radii:** Dark store delivery perimeters were contracted from 3.5 km to 1.8 km.
3. **Fleet Absenteeism:** Daily rider attendance dipped by 28% to 35% during heavy precipitation windows.
4. **Service Level Agreement (SLA) Waiver:** The internal 15-minute quick commerce delivery SLA was officially suspended on days with rainfall exceeding 50mm.

## Business & Data Interpretation Notes
- **Delivery Duration Metric:** Average delivery times in affected zones climbed from 13.2 minutes to 36.8 minutes.
- **Delay Surcharges & Customer Compensation:** Approximately 18% of orders during this two-month window triggered automated customer compensation coupons.
- **Customer Ratings:** Delivery satisfaction ratings in Mumbai and Delhi plunged from 4.7 stars to 3.2 stars during the affected weeks.
- **Cancelled Orders:** Order cancellation rates reached a peak of 8.4% due to extended delivery wait times.

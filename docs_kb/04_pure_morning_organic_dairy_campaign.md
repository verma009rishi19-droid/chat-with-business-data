# Marketing Campaign Evaluation: "Pure Morning" Organic Dairy Blitz

**Document ID:** KB-MKT-2025-104  
**Campaign Duration:** May 1, 2025 – July 31, 2025  
**Core Product:** SKU-DAI-001 (Organic Farm Fresh Whole Milk 1L)  
**Lead Channel:** In-app pop-ups, influencer morning routines, and outdoor hoardings  
**Author:** Growth Marketing Director

## Campaign Architecture
To combat churn among high-frequency breakfast basket shoppers, the "Pure Morning" campaign guaranteed sunrise delivery (before 7:00 AM) and offered a 30% introductory discount on weekly recurring dairy orders.

## Observed Performance & Category Cannibalization
1. **Volume Trajectory:** Organic Farm Fresh Milk orders leaped from an average of 450 units/day in April to 1,080 units/day in May (+140% MoM increase) and remained elevated through July.
2. **Customer Acquisition:** 38% of new customers onboarded in May completed their first purchase with Organic Milk in their cart.
3. **Cannibalization:** Conventional pasteurized milk (SKU-DAI-003, Standard Cow Milk 500ml) suffered a temporary 18% decline in sales as premium customers traded up.
4. **Retention:** 62% of customers who ordered the organic SKU twice in May remained active monthly milk buyers into Q4.

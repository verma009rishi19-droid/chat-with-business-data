# Corporate Standard Operating Procedure: Delivery SLA, Delay Penalties & Refund Matrix

**Document ID:** KB-POL-2025-015  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Scope:** Pan-India Dark Store Operations

## 1. Baseline Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Standard delivery SLA is 15 minutes from order confirmation to customer handover.
- Orders delivered within 15 minutes are classified as `On-Time`.
- Orders exceeding 25 minutes are classified as `SLA Breach (Moderate)`.
- Orders exceeding 40 minutes are classified as `SLA Breach (Critical)`.

## 2. Customer Compensation & Automated Credits
- For deliveries exceeding 30 minutes due to dark store order picking delays, the customer receives an automatic 20% discount coupon on their next purchase.
- If delivery exceeds 45 minutes, the delivery fee is waived, and the customer may cancel with a 100% immediate refund.

## 3. Weather Exception Protocol
- During municipal adverse weather warnings (e.g. torrential rainfall, severe smog), delivery time guarantees are extended to 45 minutes, and automated rider penalty deductions are completely suppressed.

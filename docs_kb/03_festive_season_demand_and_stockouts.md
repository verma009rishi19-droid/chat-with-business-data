# Festive Season Commercial Performance & Stock-out Post-Mortem (Diwali 2025)

**Document ID:** KB-MKT-2025-240  
**Period Analyzed:** October 15, 2025 – November 12, 2025  
**Categories:** Confectionery & Sweets, Premium Dry Fruits, Gift Hampers  
**Author:** Head of Category Management

## Strategic Objectives
The 2025 festive campaign ("Diwali in 10 Minutes") targeted high-average-order-value (AOV) gifting baskets, specifically:
- SKU-DRY-002: Royal Afghan Mixed Dry Fruits 500g
- SKU-SWT-001: Kaju Katli Heritage Gold Box 400g
- SKU-HAM-005: Luxe Celebrations Gourmet Box

## Demand Realization vs. Supply Chain Failure
- **Demand Surge:** Gross order intake exceeded pre-season forecasts by 285%. Dry fruit and confectionery order frequency grew 3.8x compared to September baselines.
- **Packaging Bottleneck:** While raw almond and cashew stocks were sufficient, our corrugated luxury gift box packaging vendor (PackTech Solutions) suffered a machinery boiler explosion on October 22.
- **Stock-Out Realities:** 44% of dark stores exhausted packaging inventories between October 26 and November 1. This caused premature "Out of Stock" (OOS) statuses during peak shopping hours (6:00 PM - 10:00 PM).

## Analytical Findings
- Despite record gross sales (320% increase in confectionery GMV in October), unfulfilled demand caused an estimated INR 1.8 Cr in unrealized gross revenue.
- Product returns and ratings for late-dispatched hampers suffered due to substitute plain packaging deployed during emergency dispatch.

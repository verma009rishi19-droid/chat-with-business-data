# Q3 Supplier Incident & Product Recall Notice: Artisanal Sourdough

**Document ID:** KB-SUPP-2025-089  
**Effective Date:** July 5, 2025  
**Department:** Quality Assurance & Vendor Operations  
**Affected SKUs:** 
- SKU-BAK-001 (Artisanal Sourdough Country Loaf 400g)
- SKU-BAK-004 (Olive & Rosemary Sourdough 400g)
**Vendor:** Crust & Co. Bakeries (Supplier ID: SUP-014)

## Summary of Incident
On July 3, 2025, internal food safety audits identified micro-bacterial contamination in batch lot `CC-2025-07A` stemming from an uncalibrated commercial fermentation chamber at the vendor's primary facility in Bhiwandi. Although no consumer illness was confirmed, the internal Quality Assurance team enforced an immediate Level-2 precautionary product recall.

## Operational Actions Taken
1. **Inventory Freeze:** All stock across dark stores in Mumbai, Pune, and Delhi-NCR was quarantined and scrapped between July 5 and July 8, 2025.
2. **Vendor Suspension:** Procurement purchase orders to Crust & Co. Bakeries were halted for 60 days pending factory recertification and HACCP audit compliance.
3. **Alternative Sourcing Lag:** Replacement sourcing through The French Hearth was established on September 18, 2025, operating at only 30% normal volume.

## Business Impact & Trend Guidance
- **Sales Volume:** Sourdough unit sales experienced an abrupt ~75% contraction in Q3 (July to September 2025).
- **Customer Ratings:** Associated bakery category ratings dropped from 4.6 to 3.1 in July due to unfulfilled repeat orders and cancelled morning basket items.
- **Recovery:** Full category inventory restored to 100% capacity starting October 10, 2025.

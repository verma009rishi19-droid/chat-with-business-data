# Supply Chain Disruption Report: Imported Hass Avocados (August - September 2025)

**Document ID:** KB-SCM-2025-177  
**Date:** September 30, 2025  
**Product:** SKU-PROD-007 (Imported Hass Avocados, Twin Pack)  
**Primary Port of Entry:** Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Mumbai  
**Author:** Head of Perishables Procurement

## Root Cause Analysis
1. **Port Clearance Bottlenecks:** Enhanced phytosanitary customs inspections implemented on imported produce at JNPT triggered average container hold times of 14 days (up from normal 48 hours).
2. **Cold Chain Failure:** Secondary refrigerated power sockets at terminal yard 3 malfunctioned during August 14–22, exposing two shipping containers (approx. 18,000 units) to ambient temperatures exceeding 32°C.
3. **Spoilage & Write-off:** Total inspection failure resulted in 100% condemnation of both container shipments.

## Financial & Sales Impact
- Available inventory across Tier-1 dark stores dropped to near-zero levels between August 18 and September 25, 2025.
- Sales volume dropped by 88% during late August and September.
- Normal supplies resumed only on October 4, 2025, following emergency air-freight arrangements from alternative Chilean producers.
